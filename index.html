Macro Context

July 2026 Market Overview

▼

July was another rollercoaster month for the middle-east conflict — the ceasefire framework from June broke down in early July and fighting resumed, only for President Trump to announce on Aug 1 that the US would pause strikes on Iran, citing "rapid" progress toward a deal. Feedstock costs whipsawed through the month, and the portfolio remains split between products still working through the spike and products where supply gluts keep building.

Prices broadly falling: Ethylene, MEK, Propylene Glycols, Aliphatics, Aromatics, Toluol & Xylol, and Methanol all trended down or flat-to-down through July. IPA held flat domestically as feedstock costs eased. Crude tumbled 5-7% on renewed US/Iran/Oman diplomacy and an OPEC+ production increase, pulling the geopolitical risk premium back out of energy markets.

Still tight / stable to up: Glycerin remains the standout — prices up 10-15% for August/September as RV antifreeze demand peaks and Brazilian imports slow under tariff pressure. D-Limonene faces upward pressure from a new 12.3% Brazil tariff effective Aug 15 plus crop issues from citrus greening disease. MEG/DEG/TEG stable to up on tight DEG balances and improving TEG supply. Propylene and Acetone contracts both settled up modestly in July on PDH outages and a force majeure at Flint Hills.

Watch list: The Aug 1 pause on US strikes against Iran is fragile — the June MOU already broke down once in early July. E-Series glycol ethers and MEA continue working through the aftermath of Force Majeure terminations as supply normalizes. Look for competitive activity to keep building across IPA, PG, aliphatics, aromatics, and plasticizers as import lead-times and freight costs stay elevated on middle-east shipping risk.

Economy: US Q2 GDP growth slowed to 1.5% and consumer confidence edged down in July as the renewed conflict weighed on sentiment. The Fed held rates steady in July. Inflation is now expected to average 3.4% this year, still above target, with wage growth trailing inflation and squeezing real incomes.
