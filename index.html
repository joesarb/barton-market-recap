Macro Context

⚠ US–Iran Conflict — Month Three (Ongoing)

We are now in the third month of the US/Iran war and the Strait of Hormuz continues to be functionally "closed" via threats from the Iranian military/government. A two-week cease fire starting April 7 did not stick. As of today, the US and Iran are edging toward a limited, temporary agreement to halt their war, working with mediators to formulate a one-page framework to restart negotiations aimed at ending the conflict and opening the Strait of Hormuz, with talks potentially coming in the second week of May in Pakistan. The situation with Iran remains very fluid and the markets will remain in flux as long as US military are present in the Strait of Hormuz. The continued closure is the primary driver behind price increases, force majeures, supply shortages, and freight cost surges across virtually all product categories below.
